Most personal sites are over-designed for what they need to do.

Mine should do three things:

1. Introduce what I build.
2. Show projects clearly.
3. Publish notes without friction.

## Principles

- Keep the interface text-first.
- Keep post pages fast and static.
- Keep each article focused on one real problem.

## What changed

I moved to a plain structure:

- short homepage
- one blog index
- one post page template with SEO metadata

This keeps the site easy to maintain and easy to read.

## Takeaways

- Fewer layout decisions means more writing.
- Clean typography beats decorative UI for technical content.
- Static pages are perfect for long-term personal blogs.
